SQUEEZEIT - START HERE
======================

SqueezeIt makes your videos and pictures smaller. It is free, it is private,
and it works without the internet. Nothing is installed on your computer.


HOW TO START
------------
Double-click the icon called "SqueezeIt" on your Desktop.

If you do not see the icon, open the SqueezeIt folder and double-click
"Start SqueezeIt.vbs". Your web browser will open with SqueezeIt ready.

Keep the whole SqueezeIt folder together. Do not move "Start SqueezeIt.vbs"
away from the other files.


HOW TO MAKE FILES SMALLER
-------------------------
1. Press "Choose files" or "Choose a folder" and pick what you want to shrink.

2. Look at the list. SqueezeIt shows each file's size and about how small it
   will get. Move the "Smaller file <- -> Better quality" slider if you want.

3. Press the big "Make files smaller" button.

4. When it says "Done!", click "Save all…" (or "Save" beside one file) and
   choose where to keep the new copies. Nothing is saved until you do.
   The original files are never changed, and no folder is created beside them.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
* Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no internet connection needed.

* PDFs are shown as "Not supported in this version." SqueezeIt only works on
  videos and pictures right now.

* If your antivirus removes or blocks a file, restore it and add the whole
  SqueezeIt folder as an exception, then start SqueezeIt again.

* To stop early, press "Stop". Any unfinished copy is removed.

* Need a copy to fit a limit (for example, 25 MB)? Open "More options" and
  type a maximum size per file. SqueezeIt makes each file no bigger than that,
  using the best quality that fits. Files already below the limit are skipped.
  Very small pictures are turned into JPEG (or WebP if you chose that option)
  so they can actually reach the limit. Leave the box empty for no limit.

* To remove SqueezeIt, just delete the SqueezeIt folder and the Desktop icon.
  There is nothing else to uninstall.
